WHAT WE'RE OFFERING

HUMKIN invites DJs to download, save, cue, beatmatch, loop, pitch-adjust and perform the official audio files in DJ sets, without any additional fee payable to HUMKIN for rights it controls. You may share links back to this desk.

We want the music to travel. Attribution is appreciated, never a requirement for enjoying the songs.

WHAT ISN'T AUTOMATIC

A HUMKIN permission does not replace public-performance licenses a venue or event may need for musical compositions. It doesn't override Spotify, streaming, broadcast or other platform rules.
