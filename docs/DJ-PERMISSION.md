## The permission HUMKIN offers

For any copyright, related right, or permission that HUMKIN actually owns or has authority to grant in the official files, HUMKIN authorizes DJs **without charging a HUMKIN license fee** to:

- Download and store the official MP3s on their own laptops, computers, media drives, DJ controllers, and backups for DJ use.
- Audition, cue, mix, beatmatch, pitch-adjust, loop, and transition among the official recordings when playing a DJ set, whether a private event or an otherwise appropriately licensed public venue.
- Share the link to the DJ Desk and tell other DJs where to obtain the original files.

This is a **non-exclusive, non-transferable, no-fee permission**, restricted to the repertoire and uses above. Other permissions and licenses not under HUMKIN's control are **not included**. It is not a representation of exclusive copyright protection in a work that may have AI-generated elements.

**The downloaded music does not require HUMKIN attribution during a DJ performance**, although credited playlists and referrals are welcome.

## What this does not grant

- **No blanket public performance clearance for venues.** Public performance of copyrighted musical compositions may require permission from rights holders, PROs, or lawful exemptions. The establishment/event organizer generally must handle applicable venue licenses; using one's own MP3 copy or receiving HUMKIN consent does not override other owners' rights.
- **No streaming-service sublicense.** Consumer Spotify is not a licensed source for public/commercial DJ playback, even when the artist supplied the songs; DJ software integrations do not change Spotify's restrictions.
- **No public remix or re-release authorization.** A DJ may manipulate official audio *within a performance* as described above; distributing new recorded remixes, mashups, bootlegs, sample packs, stem files, or edits commercially requires separate written clearance.
- **No recording/streaming/synchronization permission.** Livestreams, broadcasts, film, games, advertising, public uploaded DJ mixes, and podcast distribution require an additional assessment, including platform-specific rules and other music in the mix.
- **No resale or rehosting.** Do not sell, upload to a streaming service as your own music, sublicense, bundle for resale, or rehost the original MP3s for third parties. Share the official download page instead.
- **No warranty of third-party permissions.** If another creator, songwriter, performer, publisher, distributor or collecting society holds rights in any work, this document cannot waive them.

## Suno Premier provenance — creator report and remaining verification

The creator reports that the HUMKIN catalog was generated while subscribed to **Suno Premier**, with no remixes of third-party songs, and was developed from the outset as reusable creative material. This is helpful, material evidence and should be carried forward rather than treated as unknown. Suno's official documentation states that songs made on paid tiers belong to the paid creator for commercial use, subject to the current terms and conditions. See:

- https://help.suno.com/en/articles/9601665
- https://help.suno.com/en/articles/2416769
- https://suno.com/terms/
- https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13926209

**Specific operational check:** Since September 3, 2026, Suno's terms require obtaining a **permitted download through an approved Suno channel** before commercial exploitation of an output. A generic MP3 hosted by HUMKIN is not by itself proof of that source event. Confirm the approved download for each track; this applies even though the tracks were created under Premier. The site should not describe the Suno-creator report as an audited legal conclusion. Prior terms may have differed by generation/date; retain contemporaneous plan and download evidence where available.

Suno says commercial-use rights and copyright protection are distinct. Human-authored lyrics and other human contributions may be copyrightable even where fully AI-generated audio is not. PRO/publishing/other-party agreements, if any, may constrain direct licensing of composition rights.

The separate `docs/RIGHTS-INVENTORY.md` remains the evidence register; each track's new direct business/synchronization license status is on hold until verified. DJ promotional downloads are a narrower offer, not an unqualified certificate of venue licensing.

## Stem/WAV availability

Only the original **website MP3s** are available in the current public crate. Source-quality WAVs and Suno stem exports may be supplied later, once exported, organized, checked, and uploaded. A "stem" means the extracted separate elements; no claim is made that the current MP3s are 320 kbps, lossless, or DJ mastered. Live DJ software stem separation can be used where the particular software and rights permit it, but HUMKIN is not publishing unverified or nonexistent files.

## Practical use

1. Download the ZIP at `https://humkin.abundent.org/dj-pack.zip`, extract locally, and import the MP3 files into the DJ application's library. Use local files rather than playing them from Spotify.
2. A tracklist CSV and relative-path M3U8 are included for convenience; different DJ applications vary in their playlist-import support. If the playlist fails, import the files directly.
3. The BPM and key columns in the CSV are blank on purpose until correctly analyzed; file duration is taken from the existing catalog.
4. Ask `us@abundent.org` for stems, WAV, clean edits, alternate versions, or new distribution/recording permissions. Specify tracks, software, venue/use and desired delivery format.
