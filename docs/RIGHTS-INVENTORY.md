## Verification packet required **per recording**

1. **Source and timing:** Exact Suno generation date, account, original version identifier, and whether the generation was produced under an eligible paid subscription. Do not assume today's account tier establishes the tier at the date of generation.
2. **Approved download:** Date and channel of any Suno-approved paid-tier download being commercially exploited; retain the original download receipt or contemporaneous records where available. Confirm current product terms for the exact file and generation date.
3. **Composition:** Identify who wrote the lyrics, melody, arrangement, and other expressive elements, whether those authors are human, and whether any underlying composition rights belong to third parties.
4. **Recording/master:** Confirm audio recording rights and any Suno platform contract restrictions. Identify samples, voice clones, uploaded source material, covers, remixes, featured performers, or collaborators; obtain necessary third-party permissions.
5. **PRO / publishing / distributor:** Check ASCAP, BMI, SESAC, GMR or other PRO registrations, publisher agreements, co-writer shares, administration deals, and distribution arrangements relevant to direct licenses.
6. **Usage categories separately:** Personal free listening; personal MP3 download; link/official player embed; (a) local business public performance over venue speakers; (b) audiovisual synchronization; (c) incorporation in a game or podcast; (d) rehosting/redistribution. A `yes` in one category does not imply the others.
7. **Business route:** Document a playback method that does not breach a consumer streaming service's terms. Confirm whether all composition public-performance rights can be directly licensed for the specified repertoire, whether other venues/rights require licensing, and whether a venue's existing agreements affect a direct-license route.
8. **Written authority:** Preserve the exact signed permission and its term, venue identity, allowed tracks and playback method, jurisdiction, revocation clause, and the rights actually controlled. No automatic "fully licensed business music" badge without this review.

**Release rule:** No automated permission button or general business-music license for a track until these checks have been completed for that specific intended use. Legal review is prudent before operating a direct-license service at scale.

## What can launch without granting a new business license?

- Offer **links** to the public HUMKIN site, playable music gifts, and official iframe embeds for individual personal listening.
- Display **printed QR postcards** in a shop, laundromat, community room, or bulletin board **with the host's consent**, where people can choose to listen on their own devices.
- Accept inquiries from creators and businesses; review each intended use before issuing any permission.

The discovery-point model should not promise the business can play the songs over its speakers. It also does not claim to settle the underlying music rights: those should be independently reviewed.

## Evidence sources, limitations, and next action

Current song identifiers are taken from `tracks.json`; all twelve MP3 filenames are present in the web repository and referenced by the album player. The ledger **does not** contain billing information, paid-plan receipts, subscription records, or original work-for-hire/collaborator agreements.

For a usable first license, choose **one** song, assemble the source/download/rights packet, document all required permissions, and obtain a tailored short-form permission for one named location and use. Expand only after the first permission has been validated and used successfully.
