A SMALL NOTE
ON PRIVACY.

The HUMKIN mailing list is voluntary. Joining means agreeing to occasional emails about HUMKIN music, releases and related news.

What we keep

Your email address, the date you joined and a record of your signup consent. We don't ask for your name, track listening behavior or publish the list. We don't sell addresses or use them for third-party advertising.

Where it goes

Signups are stored privately using Supabase as a data processor. Only the HUMKIN operator should access the list. The website itself doesn't require an account or use behavioral analytics.

Leaving

To unsubscribe or request deletion, write to us@abundent.org with the address to remove. We will remove it upon processing your request. We do not currently send automatic welcome or confirmation messages.
