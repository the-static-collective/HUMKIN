3. Respect human corrections captured as owner intent. New PR titles, mockups, generated labels and GitHub merges do not supersede the specified listener experience or establish a successful deployment.
4. Prioritize accurate broadcasting and editable catalog/tower behavior over promotional README upkeep. Leave a source-pinned work receipt after consequential changes.
5. Distinguish USER-REPORTED, PROPOSED, OPEN PR, MERGED, DEPLOYED, and FIELD-TESTED. A computer process running is not proof that a phone heard the stream.

## Current owner constraints
- Primary listener control is live; Longwave and Same-Time Radio are invisible fallbacks; Road Shuffle remains a visible alternate.
- Preserve artwork and titles; actual now-playing must come from the transmitter, not invented client metadata.
- Background/minimized listening must not silently abandon the authentic live stream for precanned tracks.
- Tower editing must work on **every** track, with stable identity after drag/drop or up/down reorder, enabled state, title, metadata, and station membership.
- Compost Heap should include the whole library and give lesser-played work a turn, while allowing one track to belong to multiple stations.
- The Android client may be rebuilt wholesale when that is more effective; preserve working experience and prove improvements, not old implementation.
