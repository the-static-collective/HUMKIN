THE PRODUCER'S WORKBENCH / EXPERIMENT 001

THE LIVING
DISCOGRAPHY.

Find connections between real recordings. Prepare little on-air stories. Let confirmed broadcasts accumulate a history that can influence future programming — without mistaking a suggestion for something that happened.

1 · Hear a relationship

A connection starts as your interpretation or proposal, not a claim that an algorithm heard it. The source catalog is the 21 verified static recordings. New cloud uploads are not automatically imported yet.

2 · Station memory

Nothing confirmed on air. Prepared ideas do not count as history.

The distinction comes from our Kinship Radio producer pilots: prepared ≠ ready ≠ aired ≠ returned. HUMKIN is independent; this desk does not connect to Kinship systems or carry their listeners' information.

3 · Producer cards

Ready requires a named human reviewer. Air requires confirmation of an actual occurrence. Returned cards preserve KEEP / WEIRD / COMPOST verdicts. Re-open makes a new draft without overwriting history.

4 · Carry the show

The rundown exports only READY cards, in creation order. This is a proposal for an operator; it does not schedule the station, publish anything, or claim a broadcast occurred.

Local browser storage is not a secure vault. Don't enter sensitive personal, prayer, donor, or volunteer material. Back up before clearing browser data.
