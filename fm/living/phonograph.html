ORCHARD.

One HUMKIN song remembers another. Haunted Phonograph renders cross-song arrangements from creator-supplied stems; this receiving desk preserves their receipts as candidates, not releases.

1 · Receive a musical proposal

Import the candidate.humkin.json handoff produced by Haunted Phonograph. It must cite two existing HUMKIN recordings and carry source, output and receipt hashes. The underlying audio stays private in Cloudflare R2 until separately reviewed.

No manifest imported. Nothing is broadcasting.

2 · Held candidates

The browser remembers these on this device only. Import verifies the manifest shape and catalog references—not the remote audio bytes, source rights or receipt cryptographically. No network calls, uploads or station changes are made.

3 · The human crossing

Audition a private WAV via the R2 owner's tools; compare the source recordings, musical fit and receipt. To publish, the station operator must separately approve a new broadcast-ready encode, transfer it to approved public media storage, and register it through the existing Station Manager. Nothing here edits the live rotation or sets a public URL.
