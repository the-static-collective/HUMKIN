    Field notes

    Keep originals on your phone or computer; this loader makes listening copies, not archival masters. Upload failures leave the unshared source untouched. Exact duplicates are skipped by SHA-256. A locked screen may suspend uploads, so leave this tab visible while a crate is transferring.

    For hundreds of WAVs, a desktop FFmpeg batch conversion is faster and more reliable than encoding on a phone. The repo also includes a local offline converter at tools/humkin-radio-batch.py. Make MP3s once, then drop the resulting folder here.
