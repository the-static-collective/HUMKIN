LISTEN TO MY THREE-SONG SIGNAL

WHAT COUNTS · WHAT DOESN'T

The radio is real. The cards are practice.

Every completed hand can open an existing shareable three-song HUMKIN Signal. Those songs come from the public rotation, never fabricated recordings. The result is your personal listening program — not an on-air event, official chart play, or real broadcast receipt.

Blue Lemon request cards, server-issued Orchard collectibles and editor-reviewed Gambit programs have separate permissions and ledgers. This prototype deliberately does not claim them. A future version could offer successful hand proposals to the existing reviewed Gambit programming desk, only with explicit submission and owner approval.
