Open the private Tower
Use your phone as the key
  1. On your phone, open ChatGPT in the browser. Go to Settings → Security → Passkeys and add a passkey if your account offers it.
  2. On the Acer, open the Tower and choose Continue with ChatGPT.
  3. If your browser offers a nearby-device passkey, scan the QR code shown by the sign-in prompt and approve it on your phone.

Your phone or passkey manager saves the credential. On the Acer, use the same browser profile for your saved Tower settings.
